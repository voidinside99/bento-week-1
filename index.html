Spicy Bento
Week

5 office lunches + 6 cozy dinners, all from your freezer
Lunches are cooked on Sunday and eaten Mon–Fri. Freeze the Thu/Fri boxes if you like.
Dinners: cook fresh, or cook 2 and keep 1 in the fridge for tomorrow.
Fresh only: scallops, salmon and picanha don't reheat well.

Sunday prep · 5 bentos

about 2 hours ✿

Pack every box the same way: about 150g rice, one protein portion, a spicy shoyu egg, spinach namul, and cucumber or kimchi in any gap. Reheat 2–2.5 min in the office microwave.

Gochujang-honey chicken ×3

~44g protein w/ egg🌶🌶 spicyMon–Wed
  1. Mix 3 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp each honey, soy and mirin, 1 tsp ginger paste, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp sesame oil and chili powder.
  2. Coat 600g thighs. Marinate 30 min or overnight.
  3. Air fry at 200°C for 18–20 min, flipping halfway, until 75°C inside.
  4. Rest 5 min, slice, split into 3.

Spicy miso beef soboro ×2

~46g protein w/ egg🌶🌶 spicyThu–Fri
  1. Brown 1 diced onion in a little oil.
  2. Add 400g beef mince and cook until crumbly.
  3. Stir in 1.5 tbsp miso, 1 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp each sake and mirin, 1 tsp ginger and 1 tsp honey.
  4. Cook until almost dry, then split into 2.

Spicy shoyu eggs ×6–7

+6g each🌶 a little
  1. Boil 6.5 min, chill in ice water, then peel.
  2. Soak overnight in 4 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp mirin, 4 tbsp water and 1 tsp chili crisp.
Keeps 4 days. Extras make a great breakfast!

Spinach namul

🌶 your call
  1. Thaw 300g frozen spinach and squeeze it really dry.
  2. Toss with 1 tsp sesame oil, salt, 1 grated garlic clove and chili powder.

6 dinners

serve with rice, noodles or pasta ♪

Salmon miso patties

~40g protein🌶 + wasabimakes 4 dinners
  1. Mix 800g salmon shreds, 3 tbsp panko, 1 egg, 1 tbsp miso, 1 tsp ginger, spring onion and chili powder.
  2. Shape 8 patties. Air fry 2 per dinner at 200°C for 12 min, flipping at 8.
  3. Serve with wasabi mayo: wasabi powder, mayo and lemon.
Freeze extras raw between baking paper. Air fry from frozen 15–16 min.

Picanha bowl

~40g protein🌶🌶 dip1 steak = 2 dinners
  1. Score the fat cap and salt well, ideally 1 hr ahead.
  2. Air fry at 220°C for 10–12 min for medium-rare, flipping once. Rest 10 min.
  3. Slice thin over rice. Dip: 1 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp sesame oil.
Keep half unsliced. Slice cold and flash-sear tomorrow.

Scallop chili cream pasta

~45g protein🌶🌶 spicyeat fresh
  1. Pat 250g scallops very dry, salt, sear 90 sec per side on high. Set aside.
  2. Crisp 2–3 slices of prosciutto in the same pan and break up.
  3. Add garlic, spinach, 150ml cream and 1 tbsp chili crisp. Simmer 2 min.
  4. Toss with 100g cooked pasta and a splash of pasta water. Top with scallops and prosciutto.

Spicy bulgogi

~45g protein w/ egg🌶🌶🌶 hot1 pack
  1. Marinate 200g sliced beef 15 min in 1 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp sesame oil, garlic and ½ sliced onion.
  2. Stir-fry on high for 3–4 min.
  3. Serve over rice with a fried egg.

Mapo tofu with pork

~45g protein🌶🌶🌶 hotmakes 2
  1. Brown 340g pork mince (both 170g packs).
  2. Add garlic, ginger, 2 tbsp chili crisp and 1 tbsp gochujang. Fry 1 min.
  3. Add 250ml water with 1 tsp Weipa and 1 tsp miso.
  4. Add 1 block of cubed firm tofu, simmer 5 min, thicken with cornstarch slurry. Top with spring onion.

Smoked duck & spinach soba

~45g protein🌶 add more!spare meal
  1. Make broth from 1 dashi packet, 1 tbsp miso and chili.
  2. Add spinach, then 200g sliced smoked duck to warm through.
  3. Pour over cooked soba. Top with a shoyu egg.

New options

from the pantry haul ♪ swap in any week
Shiitake: soak dried ones overnight in the fridge. Keep the soaking water; it's free umami broth.
Shichimi goes on everything: eggs, soba, tsukune, bentos.
Snacks: the Mamemasa roasted beans are a good protein-ish desk snack.

Lap cheong chicken claypot rice

~42g protein w/ egg🌶 + chili oilmakes 2 · bento-friendly
  1. Marinate 300g sliced chicken thigh with 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil, ginger, 1 tsp sake and pepper.
  2. Soak 4 dried shiitake, then slice. Slice 1 lean pork sausage and 1 duck liver sausage.
  3. Add 1 cup raw rice to the pot with water (use some shiitake water) and cook until the water is almost gone.
  4. Lay the chicken, sausage and shiitake on top. Cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min.
  5. Drizzle 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp honey and 1 tsp sesame oil, then mix. Add a soft egg and chili crisp.
A rice cooker works too: add everything in at the start. Freezes well.

Pork & shiitake tsukune

~40g protein w/ egg🌶🌶 shichimimakes 2 · bento-friendly
  1. Mix 400g pork mince, 3 finely chopped soaked shiitake, 1 egg, 2 tbsp panko, 1 tsp ginger and spring onion.
  2. Shape 10 oval patties. Air fry at 200°C for 12 min, flipping at 8.
  3. Glaze: simmer 2 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp sake and 1 tsp honey until syrupy. Brush on.
  4. Finish with lots of shichimi. Serve with a shoyu egg yolk to dip.
Freeze cooked and glazed. Microwave 2 min straight into a bento.

Shio yakiniku beef

~40g protein🌶🌶 shichimi10 min · eat fresh
  1. Toss 200g sliced beef with 1 tsp sesame oil and grated garlic.
  2. Sear in a very hot pan in one layer, 30–40 sec per side.
  3. Season with the Oga yakiniku salt, shichimi and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Serve over rice with spinach namul and sliced spring onion.
The yakiniku salt is also great on the picanha instead of the gochujang dip.

Abalone & shiitake braised noodles

~40g protein🌶 + chili crisptreat night · makes 2
  1. Drain the abalone (keep the brine) and slice thin. Slice 4 soaked shiitake.
  2. Brown 300g sliced chicken thigh with garlic and ginger.
  3. Add shiitake, abalone brine, ½ cup shiitake water, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp sake and 1 tsp Weipa. Simmer 5 min.
  4. Add the abalone and spinach for the last minute, so the abalone stays tender. Thicken with cornstarch slurry.
  5. Pour over noodles. Top with chili crisp and spring onion.
